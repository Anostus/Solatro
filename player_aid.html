    SOLA♠TRO

    EVERYONE loves that popular addictive computer card game, and EVERYONE loves solitaire (obviously), so here’s a cheeky way to play a SOLO version with a regular deck of cards.

    Goal

    Survive as many rounds as possible by reaching the score target for each round. Targets go up each round, and you use poker hands to earn points toward that target.

    Setup

    • Use a standard 54-card deck (including 2 Jokers).
    • Shuffle the deck.
    • Track round number, score and owned bonus tokens.

    Round Structure

    • Round 1 target: 25 points.
    • Each new round increases the target by +25.
    • Draw 8 cards.
    • You get 3 plays and 3 discard chances.
    • Discard 1–5 cards each time.
    • After a play or discard, draw back to your hand limit.
    • At round end, shuffle hand cards and discards back into the deck.

    Playing Cards

    • On each play, choose up to 5 cards to make a poker hand and record its score.
    • Cards played are set aside until the next round.
    • After 3 plays, total the scores.
    • Meet or beat the target to advance. Otherwise, without a Shield, you DIE!

    Bonus Shoppe

    • Excess points beyond the target may be spent after the round.
    • You can only own 1 of each bonus at a time.
    • Bonus items carry over, points do not.

    Jokers

    • Jokers are wildcards.
    • If drawn, bank it and draw a replacement.
    • A banked Joker can join a future play as a wildcard; when used, discard it.
    • If you already have a banked Joker and another appears, you lose BOTH!

    Losing

    The game ends when you fail to meet the round target and have no Shield token. Your score is the last round number you reached.
