Pocket rules

HOW TO PLAY SOLATRO

Round

  • Round 1 target is 25. Every new round adds 25.
  • Draw to an 8-card hand. You get 3 plays and 3 discard chances.
  • On a play, choose up to 5 cards. Played cards stay out until the round ends.
  • On a discard, throw away 1–5 cards. After either action, draw back to your current hand limit.
  • Meet or beat the target after your plays are used, or the run ends unless a Shield saves you.

Jokers & bonuses

  • A drawn Joker is banked automatically and replaced. Add it to a later play as a wildcard.
  • If you already have a banked Joker and draw the other one, you lose both.
  • After a successful round, excess score becomes shop points for that visit only.
  • Buy Extra Card, Extra Discard, Pocket Points, Extra Hand, Double Score, or a Shield.
  • Bonuses stay in your pocket until used; each type is limited to one at a time.
  • Digital controls: H = hint, U or Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo, P = play, D = discard. Arrow keys move between cards.
